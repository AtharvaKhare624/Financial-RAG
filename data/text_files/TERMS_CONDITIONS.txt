B2B Payment Aggregator: Terms of Service and Compliance Rules

1. Definitions
* Acquiring Bank: Any bank or financial institution that processes debit card, credit card, or other payment instrument transactions on behalf of the User.
* API: Application Programming Interface used for secure communication between software platforms.
* Convenience Fee: The fee payable by the User to the payment aggregator / platform for using the Services.
* Payee: The final beneficiary or merchant to whom a payment is made by the User.
* Self-Financing Transaction: A prohibited transaction where a User funds their own bank account, their spouse's account, an affiliate's account, or initiates a transfer without a genuine underlying transaction with a third-party Payee.
* Settlement Account: The nodal or escrow bank account maintained by the payment aggregator / platform for processing and routing funds.
* Transaction Amount: The total principal amount payable by a User to a Payee, excluding convenience fees and applicable taxes.

2. Eligibility and Registration
* Users must be of legal age (18 years or older) and legally competent to contract. If registering on behalf of a business entity, the representative must hold the required authorization.
* To comply with Know Your Customer (KYC) requirements, Users must provide valid identification, address proof, Permanent Account Number (PAN), and Goods and Services Tax (GST) details if applicable.

3. Use of Services and User Obligations
* The Services may be used exclusively for making settlements for valid, legal, and bona fide transactions.
* Self-Financing Prohibition: Users are strictly prohibited from executing Self-Financing Transactions. Payments for rent must correspond to a duly executed lease/license agreement, and vendor payments must correspond to actual goods or services received.
* CPIN Verification for Tax: When processing GST payments, the User bears full responsibility for generating the correct Challan Portal Identification Number (CPIN) and allocating accurate amounts to the respective tax heads.
* The payment aggregator / platform acts as an intermediary payment agent. Once a transaction is authenticated using two-factor authentication (e.g., OTP), it is considered conclusive evidence of a valid instruction.

4. Settlement and Payout Schedule
* The payment aggregator / platform deducts applicable Convenience Fees and GST before routing the balance to the Payee's designated bank account.
* Payouts are executed according to industry-standard timelines (e.g., Next-Day T+1, Same-Day T+0, Instant), subject to the processing windows of Acquiring Banks and payment networks.
* The payment aggregator / platform reserves the right to defer or restrict payouts in the event of suspected fraud, pending dispute resolution, or by mandate of a governmental or regulatory authority.

5. Chargebacks, Cancellations, and Refunds
* Irreversibility: Once a Transaction has been successfully initiated and processed into the Payee's bank account, it cannot be canceled or reversed by the User.
* Dispute Window: Users must raise any queries or disputes regarding a transaction within 3 (three) hours of initiation.
* Chargebacks: A Chargeback (a demand for a refund initiated by a bank or regulatory authority) will only be processed if the Payee agrees to refund the designated amount. The payment aggregator / platform is not liable for Chargebacks resulting from disputes over the quality or delivery of goods/services between the User and the Payee.
* Technical Failures: In the event of a system failure where funds are debited but not processed to the Payee, the User will receive a full refund (including convenience fees) within 7 to 10 working days.

6. Security and Restrictions
* Users are prohibited from reverse-engineering the API, utilizing automated scraping/bots, or attempting unauthorized access to the network infrastructure.
* The payment aggregator / platform does not store sensitive card credentials in violation of applicable regulatory guidelines. Users must maintain the security of their login credentials and OTPs.

7. Governing Law
* These Terms of Service and any disputes arising from them shall be governed by applicable national laws and subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts.
