1. SBI Late Payment Charges
Outstanding Balance ₹0 to ₹100: Nil penalty.
Outstanding Balance ₹100 to ₹500: ₹100 penalty.
Outstanding Balance ₹501 to ₹1,000: ₹500 penalty.
Outstanding Balance ₹1,001 to ₹10,000: ₹750 penalty.
Outstanding Balance ₹10,001 to ₹25,000: ₹950 penalty.
Outstanding Balance ₹25,001 to ₹50,000: ₹1,100 penalty.
Outstanding Balance greater than ₹50,000: ₹1,300 penalty.
An additional Late Payment Charge of ₹100 will be levied on missing the payment of the Minimum Amount Due (MAD) by the due date for two consecutive cycles.

2. Commercial and B2B Transaction Fees
1. Commercial Rent: A processing fee of ₹199 + applicable taxes applies to all rent payment transactions processed under Merchant Category Code (MCC) 6513. This fee will not be reversed in case of a transaction cancellation or refund.
2. Utility Bills: If the sum of all utility payments (MCC 4900) in a single billing cycle exceeds ₹50,000, a Utility Payments Fee of 1% is charged on the total utility amount.
3. Wallet Loads: A 1% fee is applied to every wallet load transaction (MCC 6540 and 6541) that exceeds ₹1,000.
4. Education Payments: A 1% fee applies to education payments made through third-party apps, though payments made directly to institutions via their own portals or POS machines do not attract this fee.

3. Finance Charges and Liquidity
1. Interest-Free Float: Cardholders receive a grace period of 20 to 50 days, provided the previous month's outstanding balance was paid in full.
2. Revolving Credit Rate: For unsecured cards, the finance charge is 3.75% per month, equating to 45% per annum.
